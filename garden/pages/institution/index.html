Institutions

Field Notes
Planted: 2026-08-01

As a part of my goal to fulfill the content diversity aspect of my digital garden I want to be able to highlight local business I have recently discovered or come to love over many years of visits.

I found this definition for the word institution which I think captures what I am thinking of as criteria for these highlights:

Institution

an organization, establishment, foundation, society, or the like, devoted to the promotion of a particular cause or program, especially one of a public, educational, or charitable character.

I think that I have a special appreciation for local businesses after working in the restaurant industry and being brought up by a family that values them so much. Restaurants, coffee shops, book stores, bakeries, etc are all hard work that do not survive without serious dedication from their owners and employees and they contribute so much to their surrounding neighborhoods.
