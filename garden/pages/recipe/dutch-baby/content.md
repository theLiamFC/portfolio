In the Campbell house, the Dutch Baby was the sequel to the butter milk waffle. I started making it for friends sometime in highschool when I realized how simple and easy it was.

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1/2 cup whole milk
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon sugar
- a pinch of nutmeg / cinnamon

## Directions

1. First, warm up the eggs to room temperature. The easiest way to do this is plop them in some warm water 15 minutes before you start mixing.

2. Pre-heat the oven to 425 F with a 10" cast iron skillet inside.

3. Whisk the eggs until pale.

4. Mix in the flour, milk, sugar, and nutmeg.

5. Take out the skillet and put the butter in, returning to the oven until slightly brown.

6. Take the pan out and gently pour the batter into the popover pan. Returning to the oven for 20 minutes.

7. Without opening the oven, turn the temperature down to 325 F and bake for another 10 minutes.

## Notes

Finish it off with powdered sugar, berries, lemon juice, maple syrup, etc.
