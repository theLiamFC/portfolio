Popovers

Baking
Planted: 2026-08-10

My mom used to make popovers and I feel like they were always kind of magical. It was super exciting to see how much they rose and puffed with steam, and there's nothing like a buttery hot popover straight out of the oven on a gloomy day. For a while, we turned to Dutch Babies but recently I have been getting back into the popover.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 5 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • a pinch of cinnamon

Directions

  1. First, warm up the eggs to room temperature. The easiest way to do this is plop them in some warm water 15 minutes before you start mixing.

  2. Pre-heat the oven to 425 F with the popover pans inside.

  3. Melt 1 tablespoon of butter in the microwave.

  4. Mix together the flour, milk, and butter before whisking in the eggs. I find that doing it in this order ensures the butter doesn't coagulate some of the egg protein if it is still hot.

  5. Put a chunk of the remaining butter into each popover tin and return to the oven for ~5 minutes to melt. I find that this recipe makes about 8 popovers when using a dedicated popover pan.

  6. Take the pan out and pour the batter into the popover pan. Returning to the oven for 15-20 minutes.

  7. Without opening the oven, turn the temperature down to 325 F and bake for another 15 minutes.

Notes

I like to have my popovers with some hot berries and maple syrup. Can't go wrong with more butter either!

These can also be made savory with rosemary, thyme, etc, or you can substitute the cinnamon for any spice of your liking!
